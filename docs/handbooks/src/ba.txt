# RMS Business Analyst Guide
@ Recruitment Management System — roles, screens, permissions, business flows and how to use the system
@ Describes the version on the dev branch as of 2 October 2026 (roles plan, Phase 2). Prepared from the code and the project documentation.

> **Purpose of this document:** explain what RMS does today, who can use which screen, how each business process works, and what is still missing, so that new features can be analysed and proposed.

## 1. What RMS is
RMS is a small web application for running interviews. The HR or recruitment side keeps the lists of jobs and skills, registers candidates, and sees how interviewers scored each candidate.

|Topic|Summary|
|---|---|
|Who uses it|Super Admins, HR staff, hiring managers and interviewers. Each person has one login and one role.|
|Where it runs|In a web browser, on desktop or phone. Nothing is installed on the user's computer.|
|What works today|Login, Users and Roles, Change password, Positions, Languages/skills, Jobs, Candidates with their profile and documents, and the Candidate Status results page.|
|What is not built yet|Score entry by interviewers, interview schedules, and the Selected/Rejected decision. They are planned as Phases 3 and 4.|

The menu already shows the full recruitment workflow, but the unbuilt parts are greyed out and marked "Soon". They can't be clicked.

## 2. Roles
RMS has four roles. A Super Admin sets each person's role on the **Users and Roles** screen.

|Role|Purpose|What they can do today|
|---|---|---|
|**Super Admin**|Runs the system: users, roles and everything HR can do.|Everything: Users and Roles, Candidate Status, Candidates and their documents, Jobs, Positions, Languages, and permanently deleting a candidate's documents.|
|**HR**|Runs recruitment: reference lists, candidates, results.|Candidate Status, Candidates (add, edit, delete, profile, documents), Jobs, Positions, Languages. No user management.|
|**Hiring Manager**|Follows recruitment for their team.|Candidate Status, the Candidates list and profiles, Jobs, and opening documents; read-only.|
|**Interviewer**|Interviews candidates and scores them on 10 criteria.|Home and Change password. Score entry ("Evaluation", "Show Evaluation") comes in Phase 3.|
|No role|Not intended; a data error.|Can log in, but sees only Home and Change password.|

> **One role per person.** There are no combined roles (for example a hiring manager who also interviews). Someone who needs two roles needs two logins today.

## 3. Who can open which screen
The menu shows each role only its own entries. Typing a page's address directly doesn't get around this: the server checks the role on every request, and any page not explicitly opened to a role is refused.

|Menu entry / screen|Status|Super Admin|HR|Hiring Manager|Interviewer|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|Login page|Live|Yes|Yes|Yes|Yes|
|Home (welcome page)|Live|Yes|Yes|Yes|Yes|
|Recruitment › Candidate Status|Live|Yes|Yes|Read-only|Refused|
|Recruitment › Candidates|Live|Yes|Yes|List only|Refused|
|Candidate profile (click a name)|Live|Yes|Yes|Read-only|Refused|
|Documents: upload, replace, delete|Live|Yes|Yes|Refused|Refused|
|Documents: open (download)|Live|Yes|Yes|Yes|Refused (Phase 3: own candidates)|
|Permanently delete documents|Live|Yes|Refused|Refused|Refused|
|Recruitment › Jobs|Live|Yes|Yes|Read-only|Refused|
|Master data › Positions|Live|Yes|Yes|Refused|Refused|
|Master data › Languages|Live|Yes|Yes|Refused|Refused|
|Administration › Users and Roles|Live|Yes|Refused|Refused|Refused|
|Account › Change password|Live|Yes|Yes|Yes|Yes|
|Interview Schedules|Coming soon|Greyed out|Greyed out|—|—|
|Evaluation, Show Evaluation|Coming soon|—|—|—|Greyed out|
|Log out (top bar)|Live|Yes|Yes|Yes|Yes|

"Refused" means the user sees an "access denied" error page (HTTP 403) and the attempt is written to the server log. Anyone not logged in is sent to the login page.

**The default is "nobody":** any new screen a developer adds is refused to every role until a rule is written that opens it to the right roles.

## 4. How to change permissions
|I want to…|How it's done|Who|New release needed?|
|---|---|---|---|
|Add a new user|Users and Roles › Add user: username, names, optional e-mail, phone and designation, role, and a temporary password. RMS gives the user an ID (U1, U2…). The user must choose their own password at first sign-in.|Super Admin|No|
|Change someone's role|Users and Roles › Edit › Role. It applies at once: the person is signed out at their next click and signs in with the new role.|Super Admin|No|
|Remove someone's access|Users and Roles › Deactivate. They can't sign in and are signed out at once. Nothing they did is removed, and Reactivate restores everything.|Super Admin|No|
|Reset a forgotten password|Users and Roles › Edit › Reset password, with a temporary password. The user must change it at their next page.|Super Admin|No|
|Change my own password|Account › Change password.|Everyone|No|
|Open a screen to another role|A developer adds an access rule and the menu entry for that role; the application is then rebuilt and redeployed.|Developer, then IT|Yes|
|Give one specific user extra rights|Not possible. Rights belong to the role, not to the person.|—|New feature|
|Create a new role|Not possible without development: new role value, access rules and menu.|Developer|New feature|

> **Safety rules:** a Super Admin can't deactivate, demote or reset themselves on this screen, and the last active Super Admin can't be deactivated or demoted.

## 5. Features in detail
### Login and session
- Users sign in with username and password. A wrong password, an unknown user or a deactivated user all show "Invalid login!". If the database is unavailable, the page says sign-in isn't available right now.
- **Lock:** after 5 wrong passwords for one username from one computer within 15 minutes, that username is locked there for 15 minutes. The message stays "Invalid login!".
- After login everyone lands on **Home**, which greets the user and shows the tiles for their role. The top bar shows their initials, name and designation.
- **Log out** is in the top bar. After a period of inactivity the session expires (normally 30 minutes). A form left open too long shows "Your session expired. Please sign in again."
- If a Super Admin deactivates someone or changes their role while they are signed in, their next click shows "You were signed out because your account changed."
- There is no "forgot password" link and no self-registration: a Super Admin resets passwords.

### Users and Roles (Super Admin)
- A list of every user, active or not, with ID, name, username, role, designation and status. "Must change password" marks users with a temporary password.
- Usernames: 3 to 100 letters, digits or . _ @ -; each username can be used once, also counting deactivated users. The ID and username can't be changed later.
- Passwords: at least 8 characters. They are stored scrambled (bcrypt), never as readable text.

### Change password (everyone)
- Current password, then the new one twice. The new one must differ from the current one.
- After 5 wrong current passwords in 15 minutes the page refuses for 15 minutes.

### Positions (master data)
- The list of job positions candidates apply for, for example "SOFTWARE ENGINEER".
- Super Admin and HR can add, rename and delete. Names are saved in capital letters and must be unique among active positions; a duplicate shows "Position NAME already exists."
- Delete is a soft delete: the position disappears from lists and dropdowns but stays in the database, so past candidates keep it. Adding the same name again brings the old one back.

### Languages (master data)
- The languages or skills candidates are assessed on, for example "JAVA". Works exactly like Positions.

### Candidates
- Super Admin and HR can add, list, edit and delete candidates. Hiring Managers see the list and the profiles. Each candidate has a first name, a last name, one position and one language, and optionally an e-mail, a phone number, a source (Referral, Job board, Website, Agency, Walk-in, Other), the date they applied, and a job.
- **Job link:** choosing an open job sets the candidate's position to the job's position automatically.
- RMS gives each new candidate an ID automatically: C1, C2, C3… The ID can't be changed and is never reused, even after a delete.
- Only active positions and languages can be chosen. If a candidate's position is deleted later, the candidate keeps it (shown as "NAME (deleted)" when editing).
- Delete is a soft delete: the candidate leaves the Candidates list, but their scores still appear on Candidate Status (until Phase 3, which hides them there).
- The list shows each candidate's status: Selected, Rejected or Pending.
- The list also shows each candidate's documents at a glance: "CV ✓ · 4/6" means a CV is there and 4 of the 6 document kinds (CV, SSC, HSC, BSc / Honours, Masters, PhD) are uploaded; "No CV" means the required CV is missing.

### Candidate profile and documents
- Click a candidate's name to open their profile: details, job, and documents grouped by kind. Interviewers, evaluations and decisions are added in Phase 3.
- **Kinds:** CV (required), SSC, HSC, BSc / Honours, Masters and PhD certificates (one each), and up to 5 professional certificates, each with a title (for example PMP) and an optional issuer and year.
- **Files:** PDF, JPG or PNG, at most 5 MB. RMS checks the file name, the type the browser reports and the file's content; anything else is refused with a message.
- **Replace:** uploading a CV or certificate again replaces the old one. A sixth professional certificate is refused until one is deleted.
- **Delete** (Super Admin, HR) hides a document after a confirmation. The file itself is kept until a Super Admin deletes the candidate's documents permanently.
- **Permanently delete documents** (Super Admin only): removes all the candidate's stored files, including replaced and deleted ones, after a confirmation and with a required reason. It can't be undone. RMS never deletes documents by itself.
- Documents open as downloads; only staff can open them (interviewers from Phase 3, for their own candidates).

### Jobs
- A job is an opening for a position, with a number of vacancies, an optional closing date and a status, Open or Closed, set by HR. Nothing closes a job automatically.
- Super Admin and HR can add, edit and delete jobs (a soft delete: candidates linked to it keep the link). Hiring Managers see the list.
- Only open jobs can be chosen for a candidate. A candidate whose job is later closed or deleted keeps it.

### Candidate Status (results)
- A read-only table of every score sheet: candidate name, position, language, the 10 scores, the total and the status.
- The total is the plain sum of the 10 scores (no weighting).
- The table can be searched, sorted and paged.
- Scores and statuses can't be entered or changed in RMS yet; Phase 3 adds score entry, averages across interviewers and the decision.

## 7. How to use RMS
### Super Admin: set up users
1. Sign in, open **Users and Roles**, then **Add user**.
2. Enter the username, names and role, and a temporary password twice. Save, then give the person the username and temporary password.
3. At their first sign-in they must choose their own password.
4. To change a role, click **Edit**. To remove access, click **Deactivate** and confirm.

### Super Admin or HR: first-time setup
1. Open **Positions** and add each job you recruit for: Add position, type the name, Save.
2. Open **Languages** and add each language or skill you assess.

### Super Admin or HR: register a candidate
1. Open **Candidates**, then **Add candidate**.
2. Enter the first and last name, choose a position and a language, then **Save**. RMS assigns the next ID (for example C3).
3. To correct details later, click **Edit** on the candidate's row. To remove a candidate, click **Delete** and confirm.

### Super Admin or HR: add a job and documents
1. Open **Jobs**, then **Add job**: choose the position, enter the vacancies and (optionally) a closing date, keep the status Open, **Save**.
2. When registering or editing a candidate, choose the job; the position is set from it.
3. Open the candidate (click their name). Under **Upload a document**, choose the kind (for a professional certificate, also enter its title), choose the file and click **Upload**. Use **Replace** next to an existing document to upload a newer version.

### Super Admin, HR or Hiring Manager: review results
1. Open **Candidate Status**.
2. Use the search box to find a candidate, or click a column heading to sort (for example by Total Score).

### Interviewer
1. Sign in. Home shows your profile. Score entry will appear under "Evaluation" in Phase 3.

### Everyone
- Change your password under **Account › Change password**.
- Use **Log out** in the top bar when you finish, especially on a shared computer.
- On a phone, open the menu with the button at the top left.

## 8. Business data
|Business object|What it holds|Maintained in RMS?|
|---|---|---|
|User login|User ID (U1, U2…), username, password (scrambled), "must change password" flag|Yes (Users and Roles, Change password)|
|User profile|First and last name, e-mail, phone, designation, role, active flag|Yes (Users and Roles)|
|Position|Name (capitals), active flag|Yes|
|Language / skill|Name (capitals), active flag|Yes|
|Candidate|ID (C1, C2…), first and last name, position, language, e-mail, phone, source, applied date, job, status (S/R/empty), active flag|Yes, except the status|
|Job|Position, vacancies, closing date, status (Open/Closed), active flag|Yes|
|Candidate document|Kind, file name (shown only), file type and size, title/issuer/year (professional certificates), who uploaded it and when, deleted or permanently deleted (who, when, why). The file itself is stored outside the database|Yes|
|Score sheet|Interviewer, candidate and 10 scores|No (score entry is Phase 3)|

### The 10 scoring criteria
Work Experience, Technical Knowledge, Leadership Skill, Decision Making, Problem Solving Skill, Stress Tolerance, Educational Background, Communication Skill, Attitude, Personality. Total = the sum of all ten.

### Candidate status
|Shown as|Stored as|
|---|---|
|Selected|S|
|Rejected|R|
|Pending|anything else, including empty (new candidates start as Pending)|

## 9. Business rules enforced by the system
- Position and language names are stored in capitals, trimmed, and must be unique among active entries.
- Deleting a position, language or candidate, or deactivating a user, hides or blocks it; nothing is physically removed, so history stays intact.
- Candidate IDs (C…) and user IDs (U…) are generated and never reused.
- A candidate needs a first name, a last name, an active position and an active language.
- A candidate's e-mail, phone, source and applied date are optional; the applied date can't be in the future. A job sets the position.
- Documents: PDF, JPG or PNG, at most 5 MB; one of each kind except professional certificates (up to 5, each with a title). Only a Super Admin can remove files, with a reason.
- Passwords must match exactly, including upper and lower case. An empty password never works. New and changed passwords are at least 8 characters and stored scrambled.
- A deactivated user can't sign in. A username that appears twice in the database can't log in. A user with a login but no profile can't log in.
- 5 wrong passwords in 15 minutes lock that username on that computer for 15 minutes.
- Every change (save, delete, log out) must come from an RMS page; requests forged from other websites are refused.

## 11. Decisions made (2 October 2026)
1. **Scoring:** each of the 10 criteria is scored 1–10 (total up to 100). Several interviewers can score one candidate; the result is the average of all evaluations, per criterion and in total. Interviewers can add comments.
2. **Selection:** Selected, Rejected or On hold is decided manually by a Super Admin or HR, never automatically.
3. **Jobs:** a Job is an opening built on a Position, with vacancies, a closing date and an open/closed status.
. **Documents** (built in Phase 2): only the CV is required; uploads are PDF, JPG or PNG up to 5 MB; HR's delete keeps the file; only a Super Admin removes files, with a reason; RMS never deletes documents automatically.
7. **A job sets the candidate's position** (built in Phase 2).

## 12. Open business questions
1. **One role per user:** is a second login acceptable for someone who both manages and interviews, or should a user hold several roles later?
2. **Schedules:** are e-mail notifications needed for interviews?
. **Documents of deleted candidates:** a permanent delete is started from the candidate's profile, which a deleted candidate no longer has. Should that be possible for deleted candidates too, and is there a retention period after which documents must be removed?

> Gap references (G…) and question numbers match the project's docs/gaps.md and docs/open-questions.md, so the development team can trace each item.
